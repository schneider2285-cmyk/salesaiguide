What Is Saleshandy?

Saleshandy is a cold email automation platform built for outbound sales teams that need to send at scale without deliverability headaches. It supports unlimited email accounts on every plan, built-in email warmup through its TrulyInbox network, a unified inbox that consolidates replies from all connected accounts, and A/Z testing with up to 26 subject line and body variants per sequence.

The entry point at $25/month makes it one of the most affordable options in the cold email category, especially for teams connecting multiple sending domains. The platform focuses exclusively on email outreach, which keeps the interface streamlined compared to multichannel competitors.

Key Features

Unlimited Email Accounts

Connect as many sending accounts as you want on every plan, from Outreach Starter to Scale Plus. Spread volume across five, twenty, or fifty mailboxes to stay under provider-level rate limits without paying extra per seat or per account.

TrulyInbox Email Warmup

Built-in warmup engine that sends and receives real emails across the TrulyInbox network to build sender reputation before campaigns launch. New domains reach stable inbox placement within two to three weeks of automated warmup activity.

Unified Inbox

Pulls every reply from every connected sending account into one consolidated view. Read, respond, categorize, and manage conversations without switching between mailboxes, saving significant time when running outreach from ten or more accounts.

A/Z Testing (26 Variants)

Test up to 26 different subject lines and body copy variations per sequence step. The platform distributes traffic evenly across variants and surfaces winning combinations based on open and reply rates over configurable sample sizes.

Sender Rotation & Spintax

Automatically rotates sending across connected accounts to distribute volume evenly. Spintax support generates unique variations of each email body, reducing pattern detection by inbox providers and improving deliverability at scale.

Sequence Automation & Reply Detection

Multi-step email sequences with configurable delays, automatic reply detection that pauses follow-ups when a prospect responds, and conditional branching based on recipient behavior such as opens without replies.

Pricing Breakdown

Saleshandy offers four tiers built around sending volume and feature depth. Every plan includes unlimited email accounts and TrulyInbox warmup at no extra charge. The pricing model rewards commitment to higher volumes rather than charging per seat, which is a meaningful structural advantage for teams scaling from five to fifty sending accounts. All plans come with a seven-day free trial so teams can evaluate the platform and run test campaigns before committing to a paid subscription. Pros & Cons

After running cold email campaigns across multiple sending accounts and testing deliverability over several weeks, here is an honest assessment of where Saleshandy excels and where it falls short.

What's Great

  • Unlimited email accounts on every plan, including the $25 starter
  • TrulyInbox warmup keeps sender reputation healthy on autopilot
  • A/Z testing with 26 variants gives far more data than standard A/B splits
  • Unified inbox saves hours managing replies across many accounts
  • Sender rotation and spintax reduce pattern detection by inbox providers
  • Entry price undercuts Instantly, Smartlead, and Lemlist significantly

What Could Be Better

  • Email only — no LinkedIn, phone, or multichannel sequence steps
  • CRM integrations limited to Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Zoho
  • Interface feels cluttered when managing dozens of active sequences
  • Reporting dashboard covers basics but lacks depth for power users
  • Warmup network smaller than Instantly's, which can slow initial domain reputation building

Who It's For

Perfect For

  • Teams scaling cold email volume without per-seat pricing constraints
  • Budget-conscious startups that need unlimited accounts under thirty dollars a month
  • SDRs running extensive subject line and body copy experiments with A/Z testing
  • Outbound-first teams focused purely on email as their primary prospecting channel
  • Agencies or consultants managing outreach across multiple client domains

Skip If You

  • Need multichannel sequences combining email, LinkedIn, and phone steps
  • Require deep native CRM integrations beyond the four supported platforms
  • Prefer a minimal interface with fewer features and less visual complexity
  • Want a built-in phone dialer or LinkedIn automation as part of the same tool
  • Need enterprise-grade analytics dashboards with custom reporting

Limitations & Watch-Outs

Every cold email platform involves trade-offs, and Saleshandy is no exception. These are the genuine limitations we encountered during testing that could affect your decision.

  • Email-only platform means teams needing LinkedIn or phone touchpoints must add separate tools, increasing total stack cost and complexity
  • The TrulyInbox warmup network is smaller than Instantly's network, which means new domains may take slightly longer to reach stable inbox placement rates
  • Managing more than twenty active sequences simultaneously makes the interface difficult to navigate, with no folder or tagging system to organize campaigns
  • Reporting covers open rates, reply rates, and bounce rates at the campaign level, but lacks cohort analysis, revenue attribution, or funnel-stage tracking
  • CRM sync is limited to four platforms, and teams using Close, Freshsales, or less common CRMs will need Zapier or API workarounds

When to Choose Saleshandy: Use Cases

Saleshandy works best in specific scenarios where its strengths in volume, pricing, and testing capabilities align with your team's outbound strategy and budget constraints.

  • High-volume cold email campaigns where unlimited accounts and sender rotation are essential for maintaining deliverability across thousands of daily sends
  • Startup SDR teams that need enterprise-level sending infrastructure at a fraction of the per-seat cost charged by Outreach or Salesloft
  • Sales teams running systematic A/Z testing programs to optimize subject lines, opening lines, and call-to-action copy across large prospect pools
  • Agencies managing cold outreach across multiple client domains that need consolidated reply management through unified inbox

Final Verdict

4.5 / 5
Recommended

Saleshandy is the best budget cold email platform for teams that prioritize sending volume and testing depth over multichannel bells and whistles. The combination of unlimited email accounts, TrulyInbox warmup, and 26-variant A/Z testing at $25/month is a genuinely compelling value proposition that no other platform in this category matches on pure price-to-feature ratio.

The platform's limitations are real — email-only, basic reporting, limited CRM integrations — but they are the right trade-offs for teams whose outbound strategy centers on email volume and deliverability. If your workflow demands LinkedIn steps or phone calls, look at Lemlist or Outreach instead. For everyone else focused on cold email at scale, Saleshandy earns a clear recommendation.

Recommendation: Start with the Outreach Starter plan to test unlimited accounts and warmup. Move to Outreach Pro when you need A/Z testing and unified inbox. The upgrade path is clean and the price jumps are reasonable.
