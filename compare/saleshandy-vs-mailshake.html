Quick Verdict

Saleshandy wins for teams focused on email-only outreach at high volume — unlimited accounts at $25/month beats Mailshake's per-seat pricing. Mailshake wins when you need phone dialing and LinkedIn steps built into the same workflow.

Saleshandy and Mailshake both target cold email teams but take fundamentally different approaches to outbound outreach. Saleshandy bets on unlimited sending infrastructure at a low price point, giving teams the ability to connect dozens of email accounts and rotate senders automatically without paying per seat. Mailshake bundles phone dialing and LinkedIn automation steps for teams running multichannel outreach sequences that go beyond email alone. The choice between them depends on whether your outbound strategy is email-first or requires coordinated touches across email, phone, and social channels within a single platform.

Saleshandy: Core Differentiators

Where Saleshandy earns its reputation is unlimited email account connections paired with deep testing capabilities. While most cold email platforms cap variant testing at two or three options, Saleshandy offers A/Z testing with up to twenty-six subject line and body variants per sequence step, giving teams unprecedented ability to optimize messaging through data. The TrulyInbox warmup engine runs automatically on every connected account, building sender reputation before campaigns launch. At an entry price of $25/month, Saleshandy undercuts nearly every competitor in the cold email space while including features that others gate behind premium tiers.

Saleshandy's sender rotation distributes volume across dozens of accounts automatically, which is critical for teams sending more than five hundred emails daily. The unified inbox means fewer tabs open and faster response times when managing replies from ten or more sending addresses, consolidating every conversation into a single view regardless of which account originally sent the message.

The Case for Mailshake

Mailshake's strongest argument is multichannel reach within a single workflow. The built-in phone dialer with local presence numbers lets reps call prospects directly from the platform without switching tools, and LinkedIn automation steps can be woven into email sequences for a coordinated three-channel approach. For sales organizations where deals require voice touches alongside email, Mailshake eliminates the need for a separate dialer subscription that would add cost and complexity to the tech stack. The Lead Catcher dashboard provides pipeline visibility directly inside the outreach tool, reducing context switching between prospecting and deal management.

Pricing starts at $59/month per user for the Email Outreach plan, which includes email sequencing, A/B testing, and native CRM integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive. The Sales Engagement plan at $99/month per user adds the phone dialer with local presence numbers and LinkedIn automation steps, unlocking the full multichannel capability that differentiates Mailshake from email-only competitors.

When to Choose Saleshandy: Use Cases

Through real-world testing and analysis of verified buyer feedback, we identified the situations where Saleshandy delivers the highest return on investment for cold email operations.

Choose Saleshandy When You Need

  • Unlimited email accounts across multiple domains without per-seat charges
  • Budget-conscious cold email operations under thirty dollars per month per sender
  • Extensive A/Z testing programs with more than two variants per sequence step
  • Consolidated reply management through a single unified inbox view

Best-Fit Scenarios for Mailshake

Conversely, Mailshake becomes the smarter investment when your outbound requirements extend beyond email into phone and social channels within a unified workflow.

Reach for Mailshake When

  • Your outbound process requires phone calls alongside email sequences
  • LinkedIn touches are a standard part of your prospecting cadence
  • You need Lead Catcher pipeline management built into the outreach platform
  • Native CRM integrations with Salesforce or HubSpot are critical for your workflow

Downsides Worth Knowing Before You Buy

Neither platform is perfect. Here are the trade-offs we documented during our evaluation period that could affect your decision depending on team size, budget, and outreach strategy. The shortcomings below appeared under realistic selling conditions and track closely with negative sentiment clusters we identified in verified buyer feedback across multiple review platforms.

Saleshandy Limitations

  • Email-only platform with no phone dialer, LinkedIn automation, or social channel integration of any kind — teams running multichannel sequences will need separate tools for voice and social touches
  • CRM integrations limited to four platforms — teams using Close, Freshsales, or less common CRMs will need to route data through Zapier or similar middleware, adding cost and maintenance overhead
  • Interface becomes cluttered when managing more than twenty active sequences simultaneously, making it harder to locate specific campaigns and track performance across a large portfolio of outreach programs

Mailshake Limitations

  • Per-seat pricing at $59/month makes scaling expensive for teams with more than five senders — a ten-person SDR team pays $590/month before adding the phone dialer tier
  • Limited to one email account per user on the base plan, restricting inbox rotation strategies that are essential for maintaining deliverability at volume across multiple sending domains
  • Phone dialer and LinkedIn automation features require the higher-tier Sales Engagement plan at $99/month per user, nearly doubling the cost for teams that need full multichannel capability

Final Verdict

For teams running pure email outreach at volume, Saleshandy is the clear winner. Unlimited accounts, TrulyInbox warmup, and 26-variant A/Z testing at $25/month deliver better economics and more testing depth than Mailshake can match. For sales organizations that need phone dialing, LinkedIn steps, and pipeline management alongside email, Mailshake provides the more complete multichannel workflow at $59/month per user. If email is your primary channel, choose Saleshandy. If deals require voice and social touches, choose Mailshake.
