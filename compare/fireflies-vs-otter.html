Quick Verdict

Fireflies.ai wins for B2B sales teams that need every call auto-logged to Salesforce or HubSpot with structured action items and speaker-labeled transcripts. Otter.ai is the better choice for in-person meetings, lectures, and mobile recording where its real-time transcription and companion app shine.

Fireflies.ai and Otter.ai both promise to eliminate manual note-taking from your meetings, but they approach the problem from fundamentally different angles. Fireflies.ai was built with sales teams in mind, prioritizing CRM integrations, structured action items, and speaker-labeled transcripts that feed directly into revenue workflows. Otter.ai started as a general-purpose transcription tool and has evolved into a versatile meeting assistant that excels at real-time transcription, in-person recording, and mobile accessibility. The right choice depends on whether your primary need is automating post-call CRM logging for sales pipeline management or capturing high-fidelity transcriptions across a broader range of meeting types including conferences, lectures, and hybrid in-person sessions.

We tested both platforms across dozens of real sales calls, internal team meetings, and product demos to document the differences that matter most when choosing between them. This comparison breaks down pricing, CRM integration depth, transcription quality, platform support, and the specific use cases where each tool delivers its strongest value proposition for sales professionals and revenue teams in 2026.

Fireflies.ai: Core Differentiators

Where Fireflies.ai separates itself from the competition is in the depth and breadth of its CRM integration layer. The platform offers native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Zoho that go beyond simple note-pushing. After every call, Fireflies automatically logs structured meeting summaries, action items, and speaker-labeled transcripts directly to the relevant contact or deal record in your CRM. For sales teams that spend hours each week manually updating CRM records after discovery calls and demos, this automation alone can reclaim five to ten hours per rep per month. The speaker identification technology accurately labels who said what during multi-participant calls, making it easy for sales managers to review specific prospect objections or commitment statements without listening to entire recordings.

Beyond CRM logging, Fireflies supports over fifty app integrations including Slack, Notion, Asana, Trello, and Monday.com, enabling teams to route meeting insights to wherever work actually happens. The topic tracking feature on the Business plan at nineteen dollars per user per month adds sentiment analysis that identifies positive and negative moments in sales conversations, giving managers coaching-ready data without the enterprise price tag of platforms like Gong or Chorus. For individual sales reps exploring conversation intelligence before committing to a full enterprise deployment, Fireflies Pro at ten dollars per user per month provides an accessible entry point with the core transcription, CRM sync, and search capabilities that drive daily productivity gains. The API access available on higher tiers enables custom workflow automation for teams with specific integration requirements that go beyond the pre-built connectors.

The Case for Otter.ai

Otter.ai's strongest argument centers on real-time transcription quality and mobile recording versatility. During live meetings, Otter produces transcriptions that appear on screen with minimal delay, allowing participants to follow along and highlight key points as the conversation unfolds. This real-time capability is particularly valuable for team members who join calls late or need to catch up on context mid-meeting without interrupting the flow. The companion mobile app is the strongest in the category for in-person recording, offering high-fidelity audio capture even in conference rooms with challenging acoustics, background noise, or multiple speakers at varying distances from the device.

OtterPilot, the platform's automated meeting assistant, joins and transcribes scheduled meetings without requiring any manual configuration or intervention. For cross-functional teams spanning product, engineering, marketing, and customer success that need reliable meeting records without sales-specific CRM logging, Otter provides a clean and intuitive experience that minimizes setup friction. The platform's search and playback features make it easy to locate specific discussion points across a library of transcribed meetings. Pricing starts at $16.99 per user per month for Pro, which includes advanced search and vocabulary features, while the Business plan at $30 per user per month adds admin controls, usage analytics, and team management capabilities that larger organizations require for governance and compliance purposes.

When to Choose Fireflies.ai: Use Cases

Through real-world testing and analysis of verified buyer feedback across multiple review platforms, we identified the situations where Fireflies.ai delivers the highest return on investment for sales organizations and revenue teams.

Choose Fireflies.ai When

  • Your team needs automatic CRM logging of every sales call
  • You want searchable transcripts across all team meetings with speaker identification
  • Sales managers need to review calls without attending every meeting
  • You need structured action items pushed to Slack, Notion, or project management tools

Best-Fit Scenarios for Otter.ai

Conversely, Otter.ai becomes the smarter investment when your team's meeting recording needs extend beyond structured sales calls into broader collaboration and knowledge capture scenarios.

Reach for Otter.ai When

  • You primarily record in-person meetings or conferences
  • Real-time transcription during live meetings is a priority
  • Your team is not sales-focused and doesn't need CRM integration
  • Mobile recording quality matters more than post-call analytics

Downsides Worth Knowing Before You Buy

Neither platform is without meaningful trade-offs. During our evaluation period, we documented limitations that could influence your purchasing decision depending on your team's specific requirements, technical environment, and workflow expectations. The shortcomings listed below reflect patterns we observed during hands-on testing and consistent themes from verified buyer feedback across G2, Capterra, and TrustRadius review platforms.

Fireflies.ai Limitations

  • Transcription accuracy drops noticeably with heavy accents, overlapping speakers, or poor audio quality from low-bandwidth connections, requiring manual correction for critical meeting notes
  • Privacy and consent requirements vary by jurisdiction, and Fireflies' bot joining meetings can create friction with prospects who are not expecting an AI recording assistant on the call
  • The free plan has significant storage limitations that make it impractical for anything beyond a brief trial period, pushing most users to the paid Pro tier relatively quickly
  • No native phone dialer or outbound calling features means Fireflies only captures scheduled video meetings, not impromptu phone conversations or cold calls made through separate dialing platforms

Otter.ai Limitations

  • CRM integrations are shallow compared to Fireflies.ai, with basic Salesforce connectivity that lacks the structured field mapping and automatic contact record matching that sales teams depend on for pipeline hygiene
  • Limited sales-specific features like deal intelligence, conversation coaching, or win-loss analysis mean sales managers get transcripts but not the actionable coaching insights that purpose-built revenue intelligence platforms provide
  • Pricing at $16.99 per user per month for Pro is higher than Fireflies for comparable core transcription features, making it a harder value proposition for budget-conscious sales teams
  • Team analytics, admin controls, and organizational management features require the Business tier at $30 per user per month, which significantly increases the total cost of ownership for mid-size and enterprise deployments

Final Verdict

For B2B sales teams, Fireflies.ai is the clear winner. Native CRM logging, structured action items, speaker-labeled transcripts, and a $10/user/month price point make it the most practical meeting intelligence tool for sales organizations that aren't ready for Gong's enterprise pricing. Otter.ai remains the better choice for teams focused on in-person recording, mobile transcription, and general productivity where CRM integration is not a priority.
